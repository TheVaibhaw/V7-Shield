# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want participation in V7-Shield to be harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Expected:
- being welcoming and patient, especially with first-time contributors
- giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- focusing on what is best for the project and its users
- crediting other people's work

Not acceptable:
- harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- sexualised language or imagery
- publishing other people's private information without their permission
- sharing working malware, exploits against third parties, or stolen data in issues or PRs

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that violate this code. They may also temporarily or permanently ban contributors whose behaviour is inappropriate.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through the repository's **Security → Report a vulnerability** form (mark it as a conduct report), or message [@TheVaibhaw](https://github.com/TheVaibhaw) on GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially.

Enforcement follows the Contributor Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines): correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban.
